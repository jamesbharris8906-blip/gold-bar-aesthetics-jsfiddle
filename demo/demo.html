Botox

Month two was Botox, and it was the treatment I had been most nervous about. The team cleans your skin, marks the injection points, and talks you through each one. Lip Filler

Month three was not filler. It was microneedling, and I will come back to filler in a moment. Lip filler is the treatment where a light hand matters most. A small amount, placed carefully, can restore volume that has faded with age, define the border of the lip, or balance lips that are uneven. The team talks through how much to use, and they will stop before the result looks overdone. Swelling for a day or two is normal, and the result settles over the next week. If you want a subtle change, say so. The best lip filler is the kind nobody can point to. It just looks like you, rested and hydrated, and it lasts about six to twelve months. I did not have it done in month three, but I asked about it, and the team told me to wait until my skin had settled from microneedling first.

Facial Balancing

Facial balancing is the art of looking at the whole face, not one feature at a time. A little filler in the cheeks can lift the lower face. A small amount in the chin can balance a profile. The team looks at how your features work together, and they talk about proportion before they talk about syringes. It is not about adding volume everywhere. It is about placing a small amount in the right spot so the face looks rested and in harmony. In month four, the team looked at my face again and suggested a small amount of cheek filler, because my cheeks had lost volume and that was making the lines around my mouth look deeper. It was the right call, and it was the first time I looked in the mirror and thought my face looked like it did a few years ago.

Microneedling

Month three was microneedling, and it was the treatment that changed my skin's texture. The team cleans your skin, applies a serum, and passes a small device over your face. It feels like light sandpaper, not pain, and it takes about thirty minutes. Your skin will look red for a day or two, like a mild sunburn, and the team will tell you how to care for it afterward. Over the next few weeks, the skin looks smoother, pores look smaller, and old acne scars look softer. A series of treatments usually gives the best result, and the team will be honest about how many you are likely to need. I had a second session in month five, and by then the change was clear in photos, even if I had not noticed it day by day.

Cheek Filler

Month four was cheek filler, and it took about twenty minutes. Cheeks lose volume as we age, and that loss shows up as deeper lines around the mouth and a tired look in the mid-face. The team places filler carefully, checking the balance from every angle. You will feel some pressure, and there may be a little swelling for a day or two. The result lasts a year or more, and it is one of the most natural-looking treatments when it is done with a light hand, because it works with the structure of your face rather than against it. If you have ever looked in the mirror and thought your face looked tired even when you felt fine, cheek filler is often the treatment that explains why.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to see results?

Some treatments show a change right away, like a facial, and some build over days or weeks, like Botox and microneedling. A full plan takes a few months to show its best result, which is why the team plans in months, not minutes.

How often should I get Botox?

Most people come every three to four months, when they notice the lines returning. The team will help you find a rhythm that fits your budget and your goals, and they will never push you to come more often than you need.

Does microneedling hurt?

Most people feel a light scratching or sandpaper feeling, not pain. The team works slowly, talks you through each section, and will stop if you need a break. Your skin will feel warm afterward, like a mild sunburn, and that fades over a day or two.

How many microneedling sessions do I need?

It depends on your skin. Many people see a change after one session, and a series of three gives a clearer result. The team will look at your skin and give you an honest estimate before you start.

Can I combine treatments?

Yes, many people do, but not always on the same day. The team will tell you the best order for your treatments, because some combinations work better spaced apart. Ask when you book, and they will plan the visits for you.

Is there downtime?

After Botox or a facial, most people go straight back to work. After filler or microneedling, you may want to plan for some redness or swelling for a day or two, and the team will help you time your visit.

How much does a six-month plan cost?

It depends on the treatments you choose. The team will give you a written estimate at your consultation, and update it at each visit, so there are no surprises. Call (614) 412-4487 and ask for an estimate for the treatments you are considering.
